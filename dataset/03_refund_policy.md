# Refund Policy — Brightline Supply Co.

**Version:** v2
**Effective:** 1 January 2026
**Owner:** Customer Operations

---

## Section 1 — Scope

This policy covers refunds, replacements and credits for orders shipped by Brightline Supply Co. A customer contract can replace a section of this policy; where it does, the contract wins.

## Section 2 — Definitions

- **Delivery SLA:** the delivery window promised in a customer's contract, counted from the dispatch confirmation.
- **Order value:** the total invoiced amount for the order, including shipping.

## Section 3 — Late delivery

If an order is delivered later than the delivery SLA in the customer's contract, the customer gets a full refund of the order value. The customer must request the refund through a support ticket.

## Section 4 — Damaged goods

If goods arrive damaged and the customer reports it within 14 days of delivery, the customer can choose a replacement or a full refund for the damaged items.

## Section 5 — Standard terms (no contract)

Customers without a customer contract buy under standard terms. Delivery estimates shown at checkout are not guaranteed, and late deliveries under standard terms are not eligible for a refund under section 3.

## Section 6 — Deadlines

Refund requests must be filed within 30 days of delivery. Requests filed later are declined.
