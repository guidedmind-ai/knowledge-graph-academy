# Refund Policy — Brightline Supply Co.

- **Version:** v2 (replaces v1 of 1 March 2025)
- **Effective:** 1 January 2026
- **Owner:** Lena Brandt, Head of Customer Operations
- **Applies to:** every order shipped by Brightline Supply Co. from the Dayton and Newark warehouses

The support team works under this policy. Support agents Tom Becker, Aisha Rahman and Luis Ortega report to Lena Brandt. Karen Liu, Billing Specialist, issues refunds and credit notes once they are approved.

---

## Section 1 — Scope

This policy covers refunds, replacements, repairs and credits for orders shipped by Brightline Supply Co. A customer contract can replace a section of this policy; where it does, the contract wins. Contract C-19 (Orbis Foods) replaces section 3 with a credit note.

## Section 2 — Definitions

- **Dispatch confirmation:** the email Brightline sends when an order leaves the warehouse.
- **Delivery SLA:** the delivery window promised in a customer's contract, counted from the dispatch confirmation.
- **Order value:** the total invoiced amount for the order, including shipping.

## Section 3 — Late delivery

If an order is delivered later than the delivery SLA in the customer's contract, the customer gets a full refund of the order value. The customer must request the refund through a support ticket, within the deadline in section 6.1. Refunds are approved as described in section 6.2.

## Section 4 — Damaged goods

**4.1 General.** If goods arrive damaged and the customer reports it within 14 days of delivery, the customer can choose a replacement or a full refund for the damaged items.

**4.2 Cold-chain products.** For cold-chain products (CC-40, CC-80 and GP-5), damage must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with the shipment's temperature log attached. Reports after 48 hours are not eligible, because the product may have been stored incorrectly after delivery.

**4.3 Custom-printed products.** Custom-printed products (BX-100-CP) are made to order. If they arrive damaged or misprinted, Brightline replaces them. They are not eligible for a refund.

## Section 5 — Standard terms (no contract)

Customers without a customer contract buy under standard terms. Delivery estimates shown at checkout are not guaranteed, and late deliveries under standard terms are not eligible for a refund under section 3. Damaged goods are still covered by section 4.

## Section 6 — Deadlines and approvals

**6.1 Deadline.** Refund and credit requests must be filed within 30 days of delivery. Requests filed later are declined.

**6.2 Approvals.** Refunds and credit notes up to $5,000 are approved by the customer's account manager. Refunds and credit notes over $5,000 must also be approved by the Head of Customer Operations, Lena Brandt.

## Section 7 — Equipment warranty

Warehouse equipment (PJ-3000, SR-4 and LB-9) has a 12-month warranty from delivery. Under warranty, Brightline repairs or replaces the faulty unit. Warranty cases are not eligible for a refund.

## Section 8 — Carrier claims

When a late or damaged delivery is the carrier's fault, the ticket owner files a claim with the carrier (SwiftFreight, NorthLine Logistics or ColdLink). The customer's refund, credit or replacement never waits for the carrier claim.
