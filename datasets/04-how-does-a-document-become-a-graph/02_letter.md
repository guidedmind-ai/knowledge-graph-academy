# Letter from Grandma Rose (torn)

*Found in room 2, the parlour. A handwritten letter, torn in three pieces.*

## Piece 1 · Greeting

Seaview Cottage, Brighton
Wednesday

My dearest Tom,

## Piece 2 · The parcel

Happy birthday! Your present is on its way: a big parcel with a red ribbon. The new postman, Mr Bell, will bring it on Saturday morning. He has to see you in person, so open the door when he knocks. You'll know him by his blue cap.

## Piece 3 · Goodbye

Give Biscuit a pat from me, and tell your mother, Margaret, that I'll telephone on Sunday.

All my love,
Grandma Rose
