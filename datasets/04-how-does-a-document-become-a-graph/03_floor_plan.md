# Floor plan · No. 12 Lark Ln (torn)

*Found in room 3, the study corridor. An architect's floor plan, torn in two pieces.*

## Piece 1 · Ground floor

Ground floor of 12 Lark Ln: the front door with a letterbox, the hall, the parlour and the kitchen. The front door has the only lock that opens to the street.

## Piece 2 · First floor

First floor of 12 Lark Ln: the parents' room at the front and Tom's room (the blue bedroom) at the back. Tom's room has a window seat; under it is Grandpa's secret drawer.
