# Tom Hale's diary (torn)

*Found in room 4, the blue bedroom. Three diary pages, torn out.*

## Piece 1 · Friday

It's my birthday tomorrow! I'll be eight. Mother goes to the market on Saturday mornings, so it will be just me and Biscuit.

## Piece 2 · Saturday

A man in a blue cap knocked three times and called my name. I didn't know him. I locked the front door and hid the key in Grandpa's secret drawer. Biscuit barked and barked.

## Piece 3 · Saturday, later

The man has gone. There is a card on the mat, but I'm not going down until Mother is home.
