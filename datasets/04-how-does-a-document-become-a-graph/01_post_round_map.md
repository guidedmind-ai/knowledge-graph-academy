# Larkfield post round map (torn) · 1957

*Found in room 1, the hallway. A postman's round map, torn in three pieces.*

## Piece 1 · The Saturday round

Saturday round, Larkfield Post Office. Postman: Mr Bell. Start at the post office on Mill Road at 8 o'clock, then Lark Lane, then back along Mill Road.

## Piece 2 · Lark Lane

Lark Lane runs east from Mill Road to Larkfield Park. Houses on the round: No. 10, No. 12 and No. 14. No. 12 Lark Lane is marked with a star: parcel to deliver, signature needed.

## Piece 3 · Mill Road

Mill Road is the high street: the Larkfield Post Office, Rowe's Bakery and the Station Inn.
